Sabeeh-e-Mahtaab

Rukh-e-roshan hai ya koi sabeeh-e-mahtaab hai?
Miri vahshat ka, miri jaan, bas yahi jawaab hai

Tumhaari chashm-e-surmageen hai meykada koi,
Ke jis ko dekh kar zaahid bhi ab kharaab hai

Saba jo chhoo ke guzri hai tumhaare geesuo’n ko,
Ajab nahi ke har ik jhonka ab gulaab hai

Yeh naaz, yeh andaaz, yeh kharaam-e-naazik,
Ke jaise sang-e-marmar par behta hua aabshaar hai

Ajab tamaasha hai ishq ki is basti ka,
Woh be-hijaab ho kar bhi hijaab hi hijaab hai

Jis taraf uth gayi, usey maikhana banaya hai

Qalb-e-Muztar

Khila hai chehra tera jaise marmareen saaghar.

Uthe to josh-e-junoon, jhuke to qalb-e-muztar.

Khuda ne haseen banaye hain do-jahan lekin,
Tujh sa haseen na utra zameen par dilbar.

Bint-e-mehtaab

Parto-e-aks-e-rukh se tere roushan hai madaar-e-hasti,
Tu jo hasti hai to basti hai ye ujaad si basti.

Abre-baraan ki tarah teri zulf ka saaya hai parbat par,
Har ik boond mein chhupi hai naye ishq ki masti.

Gumaan hota hai ke tu koi bint-e-mehtaab hai jaana’n,
Zameen par utar aayi hai chhor kar khuld ki pasti.

Kaf-e-naazuk par tere racha hai jo rang-e-hina ka,
Karta hai mureed-e-kaamil har ik nigah-e-parasti.

Nazar milti hai jab tiri chashm-e-ghezal se miri,
Mit jaati hai pal mein har ik gham-e-darbasti.
